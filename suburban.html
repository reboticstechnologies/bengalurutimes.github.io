Bengaluru Suburban & Commuter Rail Overview

Suburban rail services connect Bengaluru to nearby hubs such as Anekal Road, Hosur, Ramanagaram, Tumakuru and more.

Key Corridors

  • KSR Bengaluru / Yesvantpur ↔ Anekal Road ↔ Hosur (via Bellandur Road, Carmelaram & Heelalige)
  • KSR Bengaluru ↔ Ramanagaram ↔ Mysuru line
  • Bengaluru ↔ Tumakuru line
  • Planned Bengaluru Suburban Rail Project corridors (Mallige, Kanaka, etc.).

Detailed corridor-wise timetables will be progressively added.
